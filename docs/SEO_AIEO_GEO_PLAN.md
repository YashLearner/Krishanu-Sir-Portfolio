## 1. What success looks like

Someone searching the name should find a clear personal profile, understand the connection to CoreDhristi, and see relevant work. Someone asking an assistant who Krishanu is should be able to receive a sourced answer consistent with that profile. A potential client should be able to judge his work and reach the correct contact destination.

These are three separate outcomes: being found, being described accurately, and receiving an enquiry. Ranking, citation, and recommendation are not interchangeable. Metadata does not make a model permanently learn a person. Live search/retrieval is the practical target; an assistant without web access can rely on older knowledge. No position, citation rate, knowledge panel, or completion date is promised.

A project can get an indexable page when its identity is accurate or intentionally anonymous, its problem and contribution are distinct, evidence is usable, metadata matches visible content, and readers learn something beyond the card summary. ## 10. Metadata and structured data

The companion specification provides checked draft title/description lengths and image mappings. Use the full name near the front of the homepage title; a concise description must say what the work is. Add canonical, Open Graph and social card metadata once the real domain/route structure is known. Google ignores the meta-keyword tag. [Supported meta tags](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/special-tags).

Represent the person accurately with `Person`; `WebSite` identifies the personal site. Consider `ProfilePage` when the final profile page meets the applicable guidelines. Link genuine personal identities through `sameAs`; company/client URLs are not personal aliases. For real articles, add matching author/date information. Structured data must describe visible facts, not hidden promotional claims. [Google ProfilePage documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/profile-page).

Visible questions are useful for readers. Do not promise Google FAQ rich results: Google reports that feature retired on May 7, 2026. [Google documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates). Optional semantic FAQ markup is lower priority than the biography and work evidence.

## 12. Human editorial standard

Use first person for the personal story and project contribution. Use a short third-person biography where a standalone identity answer is needed. Name the company and actual workflow, then explain why the work was needed. Vary paragraph lengths naturally. Use technical terms only where they identify real work.

Remove “revolutionary,” “seamless,” “game-changing,” generic future-of-AI headings, stacked adjectives, and repeated binary contrasts. No deliberate grammar mistakes or fabricated opinions. Preserve protected facts before editing. Review the copy in five passes: meaning, evidence, structure, sentence-level clarity, and remaining formulaic language. The provided drafts have been written with these criteria; no AI-detector score or universal judgement about authorship is claimed.
